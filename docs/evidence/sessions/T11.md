## Verification

- Chromium encoded five synthetic JPEG pages into **479,236 bytes** against a 500,000-byte target. PDF.js opened all five in tray order.
- Unit tests cover weighted budgets, the 450–500 KB range, A4 orientation, 200 DPI dimensions, and a budget that requires rescaling below full resolution at quality 0.35.
- The browser flow recovered from a 1 KB target and downloaded a one-page PDF. The displayed size matched the file’s length. Axe reported zero serious or critical findings.
- The `bug-fix` skill identified a tray test timeout during worker processing. The test still requires the edited image to replace the previous image. [Bug log](../../test/bug-log.md#t11--tray-assertion-timed-out-during-a-corner-edit)
- Frozen-lockfile installation, lint, strict TypeScript, 109 unit tests, fourteen Chromium tests through Edge, and the production build passed. [GitHub Actions checks](https://github.com/Suman-Rajak/Phone-Bridge/pull/13/checks)
- The SmartDoc benchmark gate remains blocked on [H5](../../plan/QUESTIONS.md#h5--smartdoc-sample-access-t03-benchmark).
