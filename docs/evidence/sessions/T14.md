## Verification

- Two Chromium contexts paired over WebRTC and round-tripped Hindi, emoji, a newline, and literal markup. The received field displayed the characters as plain text.
- The focused laptop copied automatically. The unfocused laptop displayed Copy and waited for a click. When the Clipboard API denied writing, the received text was selected with instructions for manual copy.
- The test supplies clipboard values, focus, and permissions in the browser contexts to keep the OS clipboard untouched. Real-device clipboard behavior remains to be checked.
- Axe found zero serious or critical issues on the laptop and dark phone. The phone fit at 360 px without horizontal scrolling.
- Lint, strict TypeScript, 114 unit tests, sixteen browser tests through Edge, and the production build passed locally. The SmartDoc benchmark still needs the archive from [H5](../../plan/QUESTIONS.md#h5--smartdoc-sample-access-t03-benchmark).
